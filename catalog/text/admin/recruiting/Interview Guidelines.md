Interview format:

Zoom, 20-30 min

(~3 min) Intro yourself (major, school, year, how long have you been with ECI, etc)

(~3 min) Intro ECI lab

give an overview of what we do,

highlight that we are looking for self-driven and creative people because research is student-ran

expecting students to be involved long-term

approximately 2h per week to work on code individually or as a group,

and responsibilities include producing final reports,

updating other materials we have,

leading an initiative,

updating and improving code,

coming up with new ideas, etc.

(If accepted, their first semester will be a trial one, and after this spring semester, we’ll do a review on extending a full offer & be able to register for the lab starting Fall 2024)

(~5-10 min total) Ask the interviewee to tell you about themselves

Follow up on some relevant / interesting experiences from their resume

Ask about coding experience or projects

While we code in Python if they are comfortable with other languages and are willing to put in extra work, that’s ok

Expectations for 1st years are lighter than for more senior students

Leave the last 5-10 min is for them to ask questions

Questions to ask:

Why you’d want to join ECI lab (especially if the person is a junior, how do they see themselves being involved in the lab)?

If you join, what ideas, changes, or initiatives could you lead / what would you improve?

Looking for some familiarity with what we do, spent some time on the website, skimmed through a report, etc. & commitment, good reasons, and demonstrate thoughtfulness

Ask about coding experience or projects

What other things are you involved in on campus or beyond?

They need to have something else going on, but not too many things that they can’t commit
