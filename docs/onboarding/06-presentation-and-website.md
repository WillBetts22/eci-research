## Consolidated presentation
File: [`presentations/Updated Consolidated ECI Presentation.pptx`](../../presentations/) (57 MB).
- The first ~30 slides explain the methodology. Don't change them unless the method changes.
- Each country gets **3 slides**. Copy the layout of an existing country (the original instructions point to India, about slide 116):
  1. GDP & ECI vs. peers (Figures 1–3) with 2–3 takeaway bullets
  2. RID / SDV: where the country moved vs. where it should move (Tables 3, 5)
  3. Greedy allocation + SOI: recommended industries and how optimal the past strategy was (Figures 4, 10)
- Replace the country's old slides rather than duplicating them. Keep alphabetical order.
- Commit the updated `.pptx` in the same PR as your report, or in a follow-up PR.

> Binary `.pptx` files can't be merged. **Coordinate in the group chat** so only one person edits the deck at a time, or send your 3 slides to whoever owns the deck that week.

## Lab website
Reports are listed at <https://kachanilab.ieor.columbia.edu/research-projects/economic-complexity>. PDFs are hosted at `http://www.columbia.edu/~sk2267/eci/<file>.pdf`.

Every member should have **editor** permission. If you don't, ask the research lead.

1. Sign in at <https://kachanilab.ieor.columbia.edu/user>
2. **Research** tab (top banner) → edit view in the banner below
3. Find the gray content box → **Edit** next to "Accordion"
4. **Edit** the second Accordion Item ("Reports for Individual Countries")
5. **Edit** next to "Table"
6. Click your country → the hyperlink button (8th from the left)
7. Update the URL to the new file, e.g. `http://www.columbia.edu/~sk2267/eci/China (Updated 2021).pdf` instead of `.../China.pdf`
8. Save the pop-up, then **scroll to the bottom and save all changes**

Original instructions: [`docs/original-instructions/Website Update Instructions.docx`](../original-instructions/).

After publishing, set the **Website Updated** column to TRUE in `admin/trackers/Country Reports Tracker.xlsx`.
