## Problem
- `data/raw/2023_mega.csv` is inflated in the pull itself, not in cleaning. Countries-only raw total: $31.52T in 2023 vs $23.13T in 2022; the Growth Lab's 2023 goods total is $21.66T and WDI world merchandise exports $24.02T.
- Reporter-level ratio of raw total to the Growth Lab total: median 1.03-1.04 in 2019-2022, 1.18 in 2023. Reporters above 1.5x: 3-7 per year in 2019-2022, **64 of 154** in 2023 (20 between 1.8x and 2.2x, 36 above 2.2x).
- Largest: Germany 2.58x ($1,686B -> $4,049B), Switzerland 4.22x, Czechia 4.09x, Hungary 3.54x, South Africa 3.38x, India 2.10x, Brazil 2.07x, Canada 1.98x, Mexico 1.96x.
- By chapter the increase sits in 87, 85, 84, 71, 30. No HS6 code is duplicated within the file, so the double counting is across rows that were summed before saving (likely a partner, customs-procedure or mode-of-transport breakdown added to its own total).
- On top of this, the cleaned CSVs double count by design: the High-complexity column ($12.66T in 2023) and the EU-28 row ($7.11T).
- Saudi Arabia (M49 682) has 0 exports in `2022_mega.csv` and $49B in 2019 vs the Growth Lab's $189B; its oil appears to be missing.
