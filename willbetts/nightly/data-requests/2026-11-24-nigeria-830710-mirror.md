## 1. Nigeria 2019, HS6 830710 = $2.096B
- **What we see:** `data/raw/2019_mega.csv`, reporter 566 (Nigeria), commodity 830710 (flexible tubing of iron or steel) = $2.096B. In 2020–2023 the same line is $0. It is 99.95% of Nigeria's *Machinery and Construction* cell (RCA 11.58 in 2019, 0.01 in 2020) and alone moves Nigeria about 22 grouped-ECI ranks.
- **Why it matters:** if this is a misreport, the lab's 2019 grouped ECI for Nigeria is wrong regardless of mapping, and so is any report or peer comparison that uses it.
- **What we need:** Nigeria's 2019 exports of HS4 8307 (and 8905, 8908 for context) from importer-reconciled data. Either:
  - the Growth Lab country × HS4 file already described in `external-data/README.md` (`raw/atlas_hs92_country_product_year_4.csv`, Harvard Dataverse file 13685110; or the compacted `raw/atlas_hs4_exports.pkl` from `scripts/compact_atlas.py`). It is gitignored and wasn't present in tonight's environment. A 3-row extract (NGA, 2019, hs4 in {8307, 8905, 8908}) committed as a small CSV would be enough; or
  - UN Comtrade *partner-side* (mirror) imports from Nigeria, 2019, HS 830710 (https://comtradeplus.un.org/), all reporters.
