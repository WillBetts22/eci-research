- **Saudi Arabia (M49 682)** reports $0.0B under HS ch. 27 and $202.4B / $132.3B / $247.7B under 999999 in 2019 / 2020 / 2023. In **2021** the pattern reverses: $212.6B under ch. 27 and $0.8B under 999999. It is absent from the 2022 pull.

## Why
The UAE report's peers are Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Qatar. Two of the three switch between filing oil as ch. 27 and as 999999 across years, so their grouped baskets (and the UAE's peer comparison) depend on the reporting convention, not on capabilities. With mirror data, the cleaning step could reassign 999999 to *Energy* for these reporters (proposal 2026-11-25 #1).
