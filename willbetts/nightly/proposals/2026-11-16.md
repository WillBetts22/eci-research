# Proposals from nightly run 2026-11-16 (P-2026-11-16-1: alternative noise models for the "essentially unchanged" band)

Source: `runs/2026-11-16_P-2026-11-16-1-noise-models/notebook.ipynb`. Each fits one night.

1. **Cell-level σ calibration** (needs `data-requests/2026-11-16-atlas-cell-calibration.md`). Compute the robust SD of the country-year-demeaned ln(X_repo / X_atlas) per HS4 cell for 2019–2022, rerun the trade-noise model at that σ, and check whether its τ* is stable across windows. Tonight τ* ran from 0.025 (σ = 0.031, from totals) to 0.255 (σ = 0.50), so this one number decides the band.
2. **Combined noise model.** Apply the rescaled paired product subsample and the trade noise in the same draw (σ = 0.10 and σ_sd = 0.196), and check whether any τ* survives. This gives the honest total SE for an "ECI rose from X to Y (±SE)" sentence.
3. **Choose the band by ATLAS agreement, not by a noise model.** Pre-state a target (e.g. ≥ 0.80 sign agreement among calls), pick the rule on 2019 → 2021 and test it on 2019 → 2022. Tonight, σ = 0.50 gave 0.818 agreement with 22 calls, and the product bootstraps gave 1.000 with 8–9 calls.
4. **Persistent vs transient trade noise.** Split the country log ratio into a fixed country effect and a year-specific part, and rerun the trade-noise model with a persistent component to see how much the change SE falls.
